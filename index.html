About SQUELLET

Most marine supplements rely on generic fish or krill oils and do not address supply-chain waste. Meanwhile, valuable cephalopod by-products are discarded. SQUELLET builds a circular, traceable pipeline to turn this wasted biomass into standardized bioactives with consistent specifications and a premium product experience.

What we’re building (pre-commercial)
  • Standardized marine bioactives (ingredient-first)
  • Documentation-ready specs (COA mindset, traceability, consistency)
  • Partnership pilots for evaluation and scale
  • Future clean-label supplement roadmap after validation

Customer Discovery Results

We conducted 25+ interviews to understand buyer and partner needs. Below are recurring themes and how they shaped our roadmap.

Top findings
Product experience matters most
People want a marine wellness option without the unpleasant fish-oil experience (odor/burps) and with consistent quality.
Trust is the gatekeeper
Adoption increases when traceability, testing, and clear documentation are present (ingredient specs, COA, stability).
Sustainability is a multiplier, not the main driver
Upcycling resonates most when paired with credible science and a high-quality product experience.
Multi-benefit positioning resonates
Strong interest in immune, gut, antioxidant/anti-inflammatory, metabolic, and emerging neuro-support framing—when evidence is clear.
B2B wants standardization first
Brands and manufacturers prioritize consistent supply and clear ingredient specifications before pilot evaluations.
Decision drivers (observed)
  1. Product experience (tolerability, no odor/burps)
  2. Trust (testing, documentation, transparency)
  3. Value (price-to-benefit)
  4. Sustainability + traceability
  5. Format convenience (capsule, powder, functional formats)
